# Privacy policy: Plagx Draft Preflight for Google Docs

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

Plagx Draft Preflight is a free, open-source Google Docs add-on by Plagiarism Checker X.

## What the add-on accesses

- **The open document only.** The add-on uses the `documents.currentonly` permission. When you click **Check draft**, it reads the text of the document you have open (or your selection) and passes it to the add-on sidebar. When you click an issue, it selects that text in the document. It cannot open, list or read any other file in your Google Drive.
- **The sidebar.** The `script.container.ui` permission lets the add-on show its sidebar in Google Docs.

## How your data is used

- The text is checked inside the sidebar, in your browser, and is discarded when you close it.
- Nothing is stored, logged or kept, by us or by the add-on.
- Nothing is sent to Plagiarism Checker X or any other service. The add-on makes no network requests of its own.
- No accounts, cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

The add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Data retention and deletion

The add-on keeps no data, so there is nothing to delete. To remove it, open **Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons** in Google Docs and uninstall it; to revoke its permissions, visit <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
